What is dual-level retrieval?

Answer complete

Dual‑level retrieval is a paradigm that processes queries at two distinct granularities. It generates **specific (low‑level) queries** that target detailed entities, attributes, or relationships in the graph, and **abstract (high‑level) queries** that address broader topics, summaries, or overarching themes. Correspondingly, the system employs two retrieval strategies:  

* **Low‑Level Retrieval** – focuses on precise extraction of particular nodes or edges and their immediate attributes.  
* **High‑Level Retrieval** – aggregates information across multiple related entities and relationships to provide higher‑order insights and summaries.  

By combining these two levels, LightRAG can handle both detailed and conceptual inquiries, delivering responses that are both precise and contextually comprehensive[C2][C3].
