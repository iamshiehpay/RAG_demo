Baseline description

Table 2 reports the results of ablation experiments that remove individual components of LightRAG and compare the resulting variants (‑High, ‑Low, ‑Origin) with the full LightRAG system and with a NaiveRAG baseline. The evaluation covers four datasets (Agriculture, CS, Legal, Mix) and four metrics (Comprehensiveness, Diversity, Empowerment, Overall) [C4][C5].

Key observations
Every ablated variant scores lower than the full LightRAG system (which achieves 67.6 %–84.8 % across metrics) on most datasets, indicating that both low‑level and high‑level retrieval components contribute to performance [C4][C5].
[C4] · pp.7–8

4.3 ABLATION STUDIES (RQ2)
The ‑High variant suffers the largest drops in Diversity and Overall scores, especially on the Agriculture and Legal datasets, reflecting reduced breadth when only high‑level retrieval is used [C6].
[C6] · p.8
4.3 ABLATION STUDIES (RQ2)
The ‑Low variant retains relatively higher scores on Diversity and Empowerment but still lags behind the full model, showing that focusing solely on low‑level retrieval limits comprehensiveness [C6].
[C6] · p.8
4.3 ABLATION STUDIES (RQ2)
The ‑Origin variant, which eliminates original text, shows mixed results: it performs comparably to LightRAG on some metrics (e.g., CS Diversity 75.2 % vs. 73.6 %) and even exceeds it on a few (e.g., Agriculture Comprehensiveness 24.8 % vs. 34.8 % for LightRAG), suggesting that graph‑based indexing can sometimes compensate for the loss of raw text [C6].
